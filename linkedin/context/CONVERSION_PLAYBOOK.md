# Preempt Global — Conversion Playbook
*Strategy Phase 2. This file documents a human-executed process (Joe, in the DM and on the call) - it is NOT read or acted on by the content-writing routine, which only needs to know the CTA line below. Kept here so the full system is documented in one place per the founder's request.*

---

## The CTA (what the writing routine actually needs to know)

One flat line at the bottom of Full-System Giveaway and Case Study Breakdown posts only (see `FRAMEWORK.md`):

> "If this is you, my DMs are open - not free."

This is not "DM me if interested." It announces we're not there to chat, filters out anyone hunting a free consult, and flatters the reader into the category of buyers who pay for things. Short-Form and Personality posts stay CTA-free.

---

## The DM (human-executed)

- **Never initiate. Never chase.** An account that never slides into DMs is the account a buyer trusts. Chasing reads as hunger, hunger reads as risk to someone with money.
- When someone messages first: no deck, no link fired back instantly. Ask one question - **"What's broken, and what did the last attempt cost you?"** - then wait. Let them type without interrupting.
- Sequence, 3-5 messages total: qualifying question → a relevant case study once they've responded → pitch only after fit is confirmed → propose a specific day and time ("Tuesday at 2pm work?"), never "let me know when you're free."
- Respond slowly and like a human. Most deals are lost by answering too fast and too eager, not by answering too slow.

## The Call (human-executed)

- Treat the buyer as pre-closed. They read the archive, decided on their own, and booked. Pitching at this point un-sells them - it reopens a decision they already made.
- Open with: **"You've read my stuff, so you know what we do. Want the invoice, or do you have questions first?"**
- State the number once, flat, with a start date: **"$X, starts Monday."** No range, no "depends on scope," no filler after the price. (Reference `PREEMPT_CONTEXT.md` for current, accurate pricing before quoting - $2,750/mo Simple Retainer, $5,000/mo+ Custom Retainer, $1,375 Simple Single Review, as of the current site.)
- Then silence. The few seconds after stating the price is where amateurs start negotiating against themselves - don't fill it.
- A buyer who decided alone on the strength of the archive defends that decision to his own team afterward. A buyer who was talked into it looks for the exit before the invoice clears.

## Deep resources (when Preempt makes them)

- One pain point, one resource, value extractable in under 20 minutes, framed as internal work product (e.g. a real findings-report excerpt, not a polished lead magnet).
- Delivered personally in DM, never through an automated opt-in funnel. The resource builds trust; the human conversation is what books the call.
- Embed a case study matching the reader's exact situation where possible, so that by the time they finish it, they feel Preempt understands their project better than the people they've already paid.

---

## What this playbook explicitly rules out

- Automated DM sequences, chatbots, or delegated conversations with buyers - the human in the DM is the product, and this is the one thing that does not scale even as the system grows (`STRATEGY.md` Phase 3).
- Instant replies, follow-up pressure, or "just checking in" nudges.
- Pitching or re-explaining the offer to a buyer who already booked off the strength of the content archive.
