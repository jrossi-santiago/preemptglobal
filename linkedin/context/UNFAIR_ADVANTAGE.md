# Preempt Global — Unfair Advantage
*Strategy Phase 0, #2. Read before choosing a topic from `FRAMEWORK.md`'s topic bank.*

## The filter rule

**If a competitor with no access to Preempt's findings data or Joe's field experience could have written this exact post, it doesn't publish as-is.** A post about "why change orders happen" that any construction-content account could produce is reach with no moat. Layer in one of the advantages below before it goes out - a real number, a specific pattern, or a founder detail nobody else can claim.

## What the advantage actually is

- **Founder field experience, not a certification.** Joe spent five-plus years producing construction documents and managing project administration on jobs up to $60 million before founding Preempt. The credibility basis is "I drew these sets and know where the bodies are buried" - not a credential, not a tool, not a franchise.
- **Reviewers who drew these sets themselves.** Never outsourced, never a black box. Most competitors selling QA/QC review are software (clash-detection tools) or generalist reviewers without hands-on drafting experience on the same disciplines they're reviewing.
- **A findings report with a number attached to every single item.** Sheet-located, severity-ranked (High/Med/Low), dollar exposure and schedule impact per finding - not a general comment list. Competitors hand back comments; Preempt hands back a ranked total. This is the one structural detail the swipe-pattern data (`SWIPE_PATTERNS.md`) already shows outperforms everything else when paired with a CTA.
- **A financial guarantee, not a promise.** The $50K-exposure-or-free guarantee is real money on the line if the review doesn't surface at least $50,000 in documented cost exposure. Very few competitors will put a dollar figure behind their own accuracy.
- **Volume of reviewed sets across project types.** Every review adds to a pattern library (recurring clash types, recurring spec-vs-drawing mismatches, recurring document-control failures) that a single in-house team or a first-time reviewer doesn't have access to. This is what makes Format 3 (Short-Form High-Signal) posts credible instead of generic - the pattern has to come from something Preempt has actually seen at scale, not a plausible-sounding guess.

## Applying the filter before writing

Ask, for any topic pulled from the bank: "Could a generic construction-content account post this same angle tomorrow?" If yes, it needs one of:
1. A real number from a finding, review, or category stat (`BRAND_VOICE.md` Section 1) that a generalist account doesn't have.
2. A specific pattern only visible across volume ("half the items on a typical punch list are clashes that were already sitting in the drawings" - a claim that requires having actually reviewed many sets to make credibly).
3. A founder-specific memory or scar ("the fire-rated wall value-engineered out of Corridor B") rather than a hypothetical example.

If none of those three fit naturally, the topic isn't ready to write yet - either dig for the real detail first, or pick a different topic from the bank.
