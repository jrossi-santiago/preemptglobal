PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: July 27, 2026 · Preempt Global

Preempt Global ("Preempt Global," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our construction document review services.

By accessing our website or using our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

01

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect the following categories of information.

Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide:

  • –Name
  • –Company name
  • –Email address
  • –Phone number
  • –Mailing address
  • –Project information
  • –Construction documents
  • –Drawings
  • –Specifications
  • –Contracts
  • –RFIs
  • –Submittals
  • –Other project-related files
  • –Messages submitted through our contact forms

Payment Information

If you purchase services from us, payment information is processed through our third-party payment processor. We do not store your complete credit card or banking information on our servers.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • –IP address
  • –Browser type
  • –Device information
  • –Operating system
  • –Pages visited
  • –Referral source
  • –Date and time of access
  • –General usage statistics

This information helps us improve our website and services.

02

COOKIES AND ANALYTICS

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality and analyze website traffic.

These technologies may include:

  • –Essential cookies
  • –Performance cookies
  • –Analytics cookies

We may use services such as Google Analytics or similar providers to understand how visitors use our website.

Most browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

03

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:

  • –Respond to inquiries
  • –Provide construction document review services
  • –Analyze uploaded project documentation
  • –Generate reports and recommendations
  • –Improve our services
  • –Process payments
  • –Communicate regarding projects
  • –Provide customer support
  • –Maintain website security
  • –Comply with legal obligations
04

USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Preempt Global utilizes artificial intelligence ("AI") tools to assist with certain aspects of our services.

AI may be used to:

  • –Analyze construction documents
  • –Identify potential inconsistencies
  • –Assist in document review
  • –Generate summaries
  • –Improve efficiency
  • –Assist with customer support communications

AI-generated findings are reviewed as part of our professional workflow and are intended to assist—not replace—professional judgment.

Uploaded documents may be processed using trusted third-party AI service providers. We make reasonable efforts to work with providers that maintain appropriate security and privacy practices.

05

FILE UPLOADS

Clients may upload project files for review.

These files may include confidential business information, architectural drawings, engineering documents, specifications, contracts, schedules, and related project materials.

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect uploaded files from unauthorized access.

Please avoid uploading information that is unrelated to your project or that you are not authorized to share.

06

SHARING OF INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted third parties that help us operate our business, including:

  • –Payment processors
  • –Cloud storage providers
  • –Analytics providers
  • –AI technology providers
  • –IT and security service providers

These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform their services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

07

DATA SECURITY

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08

DATA RETENTION

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • –Provide our services
  • –Maintain business records
  • –Resolve disputes
  • –Enforce agreements
  • –Comply with legal obligations

After that period, information may be securely deleted or anonymized.

09

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or utilize third-party services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

We encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

10

YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • –Request access to your personal information
  • –Request correction of inaccurate information
  • –Request deletion of certain information
  • –Object to certain processing activities
  • –Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

11

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our website and services are intended solely for businesses and professionals.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

12

UNITED STATES ONLY

Our services are intended for businesses operating within the United States.

If you access our website from outside the United States, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

13

CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.

Continued use of our website after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

14

CONTACT US

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us.
