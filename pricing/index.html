Before You Ask

QUESTIONS ABOUT PRICING?
HERE'S THE STRAIGHT ANSWER.

How is Preempt Global priced? + –

Two ways: a monthly retainer for unlimited set reviews ($7,000/mo), or a single one-time review ($9,000 per review). Every deliverable starts included on both — uncheck the ones you don't need to bring the price down. Custom packages are available for anything that doesn't fit either.

What's included in every review? + –

A full review of the issued set across every discipline, and a written findings report — every conflict located by sheet and detail, severity-ranked, with a dollar exposure and schedule impact on each. Those two can't be removed. Everything else — the live review meeting, AHJ submission handling, revision re-review, a dedicated point of contact, and a cumulative findings tracker — starts included and can be unchecked to adjust price and scope.

What happens if the review doesn't find $50,000 in exposure? + –

You don't pay for it. No partial credit, no fine print, on either offer. If the review doesn't surface at least $50,000 in documented cost exposure, the review is free.

Should I go on retainer or book a one-time review? + –

The retainer is the lower cost per review and covers unlimited sets across every active project, so it's the default for anyone with more than one review coming. One-time is built for a single issued set that needs one clean pass and nothing ongoing.

Can I cancel the retainer? + –

Yes — pause or cancel anytime. The one-time review is a single engagement, not a retainer, so there's nothing to cancel once it's delivered.

Is there a minimum project size to work with Preempt Global? + –

No. There's no minimum construction value on either offer. The deciding factor is coordination complexity — any set with multiple trades sharing tight coordination is a fit, regardless of project size. See the full breakdown on what a construction document review actually catches.
